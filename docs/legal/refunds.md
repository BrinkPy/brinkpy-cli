# Refund Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Brinkpy is free to use at this time. This policy applies once paid plans are available (see [Terms of Service](terms.md#free-access-and-paid-plans)).

## Try Before You Pay

Every new user can use Brinkpy free for 14 days from their first run, without a payment method. We encourage you to use the trial to decide whether Brinkpy fits your work before you subscribe.

## Canceling

You can cancel a subscription at any time, from the subscription management page that Stripe provides (open it with the CLI or from the link in your receipt). Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You keep access until then, and you are not charged again.

## Refunds

- **First payment.** If you are not satisfied, you can ask for a full refund of the first payment of a subscription, monthly or yearly, within 14 days of that payment.
- **Yearly renewals.** You can ask for a full refund of a yearly renewal within 14 days of the renewal payment, if you have not used Brinkpy since the renewal.
- **Other payments.** Monthly renewals, and partial periods after cancellation, are not refunded, except where the law requires it or where a billing error occurred.
- **Billing errors.** If you were charged in error, for example twice for the same period, we will refund the extra charge.
- **If we stop offering Brinkpy,** we refund any prepaid time you cannot use.

To ask for a refund, write to brinkpy.app@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the date of the payment. Refunds are issued through Stripe to the original payment method, and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

This policy does not take away any right to a refund or to withdraw from a purchase that you have under the consumer laws of the country where you live.
